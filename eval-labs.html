🧩 Playbook 🛠️ ML Lab

Rigged labs. Your job: catch the rigging.

Every lab is a small ML project whose evaluation looks great and is lying. Flaws are planted; a sealed grader inside each notebook knows the answer key. You investigate, name the flaws, fix them, and report the honest number. You pass when you catch everything — not when time runs out.

1 · Open in ColabRun the sealed grader, then the suspicious pipeline. Save a copy in Drive to work.
2 · Find the liesInvestigate, then submit_verdict(...). Instant found/missed feedback; debrief unlocks on pass.
3 · Record the passPaste the completion code here, and drop your notebook in the repo for review.
Labs are armed one at a time — when you pass one, ask Claude to rig the next.
